"How do attention scaling factors in Vaswani et al. mitigate gradient vanishing, and how does BERT adapt this for bidirectional representation?"

Multi-Source Synthesis 100% SOURCED

Vaswani et al. introduce a variance normalization divisor of √d_k open_in_new to combat vanishing gradients when inner product magnitudes push the softmax into regions with steep saturation [1: Vaswani, p.4].

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax((Q KT) / √dk) V

BERT preserves this identical scaled dot-product implementation across all self-attention heads, but replaces the causal directional masking with unconditional bidirectional cross-token self-attention [2: Devlin, p.3].
