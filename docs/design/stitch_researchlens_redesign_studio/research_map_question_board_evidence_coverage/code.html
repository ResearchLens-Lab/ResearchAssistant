Mathematical Scaling & Dot-Product Saturation (1/√d_k factor)

Examines how scaling the dot products by the square root of projection dimension d_k counters extreme softmax gradient vanishing in multi-head spaces.

Corpus Evidence Extracts 2 Equations Verified
Vaswani et al. (2017) Page 4, Equation (1)
check_circle Verified Eq
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax((Q K^T) / sqrt(d_k)) * V

"We suspect that for large values of d_k, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax function into regions where it has extremely small gradients."

Devlin et al. (2018) Section 3.1 & Architecture
check_circle Verified Arch

BERT Base and Large strictly retain d_k = 64 across 12 and 16 heads respectively to maintain variance stability during pre-training cross-entropy passes.

02 Attention Topology Fully Grounded (2 Sources)

Bidirectional Causal Masking Elimination vs Autoregressive Bounds

Contrasting Vaswani's lower-triangular causal attention matrix against Devlin's unconstrained bidirectional attention enabled by the Masked LM pre-training objective.

Devlin et al. §3.1 (p. 2):

Unlike OpenAI GPT which uses a left-to-right Transformer where every token can only attend to previous tokens in the self-attention layers, BERT's masked tokens allow bidirectional conditioning across the entire sequence.
