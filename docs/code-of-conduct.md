# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a friendly, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in the **ResearchLens** project, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Standards and Expectations

We ask all contributors and maintainers to uphold the following standards:

- **Constructive Communication**: Focus on constructive feedback, technical merit, and collaborative problem-solving.
- **Respectful Collaboration**: Treat fellow contributors, maintainers, and users with professional respect and patience.
- **Academic Integrity**: Uphold high standards for reproducibility, accurate documentation, and transparent provenance in research-focused code.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in our spaces:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information of others without permission.
- Disruptive or adversarial behavior that harms team collaboration.

## Enforcement and Inquiries

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. If you observe unacceptable behavior, please reach out to the project lead at `goravgumber@gmail.com`. All concerns will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
