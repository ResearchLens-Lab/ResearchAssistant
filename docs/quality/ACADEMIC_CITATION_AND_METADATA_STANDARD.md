# ResearchLens: Academic Citation & Metadata Standard
**Document Reference:** `RL-SPEC-CITATION-002`  
**Status:** Approved & Enforced  
**Classification:** Scholarly Communication & Provenance Verification  

---

## 1. Objective & Scope

In academic research systems, citations are not stylistic decorations; they are the bedrock of intellectual integrity and verifiability. ResearchLens enforces strict academic citation standards across all query answers, UI badges, tooltips, and exported research briefs.

---

## 2. Authoritative Citation Labeling Standard

All inline and tabular citations are deterministically formatted according to standard academic conventions:

| Author Count / Metadata | Standard Citation Format | Concrete Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Author** | `[LastName, Year, p. X]` | `[Smith, 2024, p. 14]` |
| **Two Authors** | `[LastName1 & LastName2, Year, p. X]` | `[Cormen & Leiserson, 2022, p. 38]` |
| **Three or More Authors** | `[LastName1 et al., Year, pp. X-Y]` | `[Hassan et al., 2025, pp. 2-3]` |
| **No Authors (Title Fallback)** | `["Short Title", Year, p. X]` | `["Benchmark Analysis", 2025, p. 10]` |
| **Multi-page Excerpt** | `[Authors, Year, pp. Start-End]` | `[Vance et al., 2025, pp. 12-15]` |
| **No Year Available** | `[Authors, p. X]` | `[Smith, p. 1]` |

## 3. Provenance Verification Contract (`SourceCitation`)

Every citation emitted by the deterministic engine maps 1:1 to a verified `SourceCitation` record in the API payload:

```json
{
  "source_id": "S1",
  "paper_id": "5d009428-7a4c-4225-9878-ae252140b1c3",
  "document_id": "38c39565-1e4f-4426-a313-c613485deffb",
  "chunk_id": "5d009428-7a4c-4225-9878-ae252140b1c3_c0007_p2-3",
  "page_start": 2,
  "page_end": 3,
  "section": "4. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY",
  "title": "Advances in Cybersecurity: A Literature Review",
  "citation_label": "[Hassan et al., 2025, pp. 2-3]",
  "author_metadata": ["Amrani Hassan", "Kennedy Hadullo", "Kelvin Tole"],
  "publication_year": 2025,
  "evidence_excerpt": "4. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY This study employs a systematic literature review..."
}
```

---

## 4. Anti-Hallucination Invariants

1. **Zero Phantom Citation Keys:** An answer cannot contain `[S9]` unless `S9` exists in the retrieved source collection. Any unmapped citation tag causes `EvidenceValidator` to flag the claim as unsupported.
2. **Deterministic Label Derivation:** Citation labels are computed programmatically from verified document metadata and physical chunk page bounds; they are never generated by language model completion.
3. **One-Click Deep Linking:** Clicking a `CitationBadge` in the frontend triggers two synchronous actions:
   - Scrolls the Evidence Drawer to the matching passage.
   - Launches the authenticated PDF reader positioned directly on the cited physical page.
